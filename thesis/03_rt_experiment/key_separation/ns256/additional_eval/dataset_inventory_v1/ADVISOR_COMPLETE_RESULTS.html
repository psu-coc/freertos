Sonar and Wheel results present in the repository

Fixed DMR is the audited job-identity result. The sliding column is the one-period window w_start = s[i−1] + P, w_end = w_start + P, counted only for a job whose immediate nominal predecessor has an actual start. The first job is not in that ratio. Unexecuted jobs stay in the fixed numerator and are not given a finish.

Sonar at 100 Hz and Sonar at 1000 Hz are not measured. Wheel at those rates is Wheel-only. The 10 Hz rows are one concurrent experiment with a separate timestamp record for each task. An older 60 s pilot records Sonar at 5 Hz and Wheel at 1 Hz. It is not one of these six tables.

In dmr_10Hz10Hz_original_B1024_r1_formal_dmr.csv, Sonar job 0 finishes at 2032451 µs and Wheel job 0 finishes at 2030509 µs.

The first advisor HTML assigns the Sonar 10 Hz detail rows to Wheel 10 Hz in JavaScript (yes). That display matches the run-1 DMR values. It is not a second measurement, and it is not evidence that the Wheel rows were absent from the CSV.
