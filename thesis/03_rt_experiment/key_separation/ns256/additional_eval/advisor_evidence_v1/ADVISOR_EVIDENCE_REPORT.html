B. FAR comparison

The archived logs store Runtime as an integer millisecond. That value is floor(TIM2 difference × 1000 / 137500). The raw TIM2 counter is not in the log, so the unused part of the last millisecond cannot be recovered. One TIM2 tick is 1/137500 s, about 7.27 µs. The remaining uncertainty is strictly less than 1 ms.

FAR_original = actual / floor(f × T_ms / 1000). FAR_continuous = actual / (f × T_ms / 1000). Each reported mean is the mean of ten per-round ratios, which is the campaign aggregate. It is not the ratio of the summed counts. Values above 1 are kept. This is not a nominal-job fulfillment ratio.

C. Deadline comparison

Fixed formal DMR is the audited result. The other column is the exploratory sliding-reference miss ratio. For a nominally consecutive pair, d_slide = s[i−1] + 2P, and the pair misses when the finish is after that instant. The first lattice job is excluded. A job with no valid immediate predecessor is excluded. An unexecuted obligation is counted separately and is not treated as on time. This is not a standard soft-deadline DMR, and it does not replace the fixed result.

D. Inter-start jitter

The plotted statistic is the arithmetic mean of |s[k] − s[k−1] − P|, in microseconds. The first in-window start, and the interval that touches it, is dropped. The standard deviation in the CSV is the population standard deviation, divided by the number of pairs. The 95th percentile is the linear nearest-rank percentile from rt_metrics_wall30.py. Whiskers are the minimum and maximum of distinct-capture means. They are not a confidence interval.

Wheel 100 Hz and Wheel 1000 Hz have three files and one distinct start sequence per configuration. Those files are not averaged as three independent measurements. The UART field inter_start avg is the mean interval itself, not |Δs − P|. The dashed line on each panel is the period.

E. Advisor decision page

Three options are open. This report does not rank them.

คำอธิบาย. หน้านี้ให้เลือก ไม่ได้เลือกให้ ยังไม่แก้ต้นฉบับจนกว่าจะมีการยืนยัน

Option 1 — Recalculated FAR, jitter, and fixed formal DMR

Strength. FAR uses the recorded duration without an integer job-count denominator. Jitter describes start spacing. Fixed DMR remains the audited deadline result, including late, unexecuted, and right-censored jobs.

Limitation. FAR is still quantized to 1 ms. At 1000 Hz the two FAR formulas are identical for that reason. Jitter comes from the Gate 3 captures, not from the DMR recapture.

Manuscript implication. A later revision would replace 10 Hz FAR values that used the floored expectation, and would keep fixed DMR as the deadline claim. Sliding would not appear.

Option 2 — Option 1 plus the sliding-reference ratio as a supplement

Strength. The supplement shows that jobs which do run can remain about one period apart after the original schedule has already been missed.

Limitation. The ratio looks healthy when the unexecuted tail is removed from its denominator. It must not be labeled formal DMR or a standard soft deadline.

Manuscript implication. Any supplement would have to print eligible, excluded, and unexecuted counts beside the ratio. The fixed DMR table would stay the deadline result.

Option 3 — Another deadline definition, to be specified

Strength. The advisor can name the reference instant and the miss rule before any further claim is written.

Limitation. The present fixed and sliding numbers would remain records of those two definitions. They would not be renamed after the fact.

Manuscript implication. No deadline paragraph should be revised until that definition is written down. This directory does not invent one.

F. Evidence and limitations

  • FAR rounds: freertos_rt/FarSweep_NS256_{10,100,1000}Hz_A/analysis/, 69 configurations, 10 rounds each.
  • Fixed DMR: additional_eval/dmr/final/FINAL_DMR_RESULTS.csv, recomputed in memory-equivalent form to recomputed_formal_dmr.csv.
  • Sliding inputs: additional_eval/dmr/raw and additional_eval/dmr/recapture/raw. Prior sliding tables in post_advisor_bc_v1 were compared and not overwritten.
  • Jitter inputs: Gate 3 WheelSonar10Hz_NS256_A, Wheel100Hz_NS256_A, and Wheel1000Hz_NS256_A.
  • FAR logs do not contain the raw TIM2 difference.
  • High-rate DMR and jitter captures are repeated files, not three independent timing outcomes.
  • The nine Gate 3 coverage-matrix jitter cells were rounded or verbal. Exact recomputed means are below. A mismatch with a rounded cell is reported and does not change the exact mean.
  • No Zephyr measurement is included or inferred.
